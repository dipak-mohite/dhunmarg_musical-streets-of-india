# 🎵 Dhunmarg — Musical Streets of India

> **Walk through India. Discover the places. Hear their stories through music.**

Dhunmarg is an immersive, interactive musical world where **music lives inside the places you discover**.

Instead of browsing a traditional playlist, you explore a vibrant Indian city in first person. Walk through its streets, discover a truck, step into a barber shop, visit a chai tapri, enter a bus, explore a corporate office, find an energetic DJ Baraat, experience a concert or sit inside an atmospheric Mahfil — and each place has its own musical identity.

**The world itself becomes the music player.**

---

## 🌆 The Experience

Imagine walking through an Indian street at dusk.

You hear the atmosphere of the city around you.

You notice a decorated Indian truck.

You walk closer.

🎵 **The truck's music starts.**

You continue walking and discover a barber shop.

You enter.

🎵 **The barber shop playlist takes over.**

You walk outside and find a chai tapri glowing under warm lights.

🎵 **A completely different musical experience begins.**

Further down the road you discover an Indian bus, an auto-rickshaw, a corporate office, a wedding Baraat, a concert and a Mahfil.

Every place has its own sound.

There is no need to browse through endless playlists.

**You discover music by exploring the world.**
